About 15 minutes · laptop or desktop keyboard

Help test a stress detector that reads typing rhythm, not words

You'll type short answers to everyday questions: first relaxed, then under time pressure with quick maths checks. The page measures how you type, then gives you a code to send back.

What is kept

  • Timing numbers: how long keys are held, gaps between keys, pauses, backspace rate
  • Your 1–10 stress ratings and multiple-choice survey answers
  • Your participant code and which session this is

What is never kept

  • Anything you type. Text stays in this page and is erased
  • Your name, email, or which keys you pressed
  • Your maths answers

Turn your sound on. The pressure round turns the screen red and uses ticking and beeps. If that would be a problem for you (for example, sensitivity to flashing or loud sounds), please don't take part.

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing this page, and nothing is sent unless you send the code yourself. The pressure round is designed to be hard; nobody sees how well you did.

This study needs a laptop or desktop keyboard. Phone and tablet keyboards don't report key timing, so please open this file on a laptop.
Use the code the researcher gave you, not your name.
